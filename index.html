Bharat SecureHealth · Policy WordingUIN BSH-FF-2026
3.2  Room Rent Eligibility
Room rent shall be payable up to ₹5,000 per day (1% of Sum Insured) for a standard single/semi-private room.
3.3  Proportionate Deduction
If the Insured occupies a room whose rent exceeds the eligible limit, the Company shall pay the associated medical expenses that vary with room category — namely nursing, consultant and surgeon fees, and operation theatre charges — in the same proportion that the eligible room rent bears to the actual room rent. Medicines, consumables, implants, diagnostics and ICU charges shall not be subject to such proportionate deduction.

  • So nursing and doctor fees are cut too.
  • Medicines, tests and ICU are not affected.
